About North Star Bakery

Our Story

Founded in 2018, North Star Bakery began with a simple belief: every neighborhood deserves fresh, honest bread. What started as a small home kitchen project has grown into a cherished community gathering space where neighbors catch up over morning coffee and warm pastries.

Sourcing Philosophy

We work directly with regional farmers to source 100% stone-ground organic flour, pasture-raised eggs, and farm-fresh butter. We believe great bread starts at the soil level.
